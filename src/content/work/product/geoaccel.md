> Poverty and wealth disparity appear to be perversely linked with progress, the rent is too damn high, and it's all because of land.

GeoAccel combines political philosophy with startups. The objective is to enact Georgist Land Value Taxes through the free market. Here's a great [primer on Georgism by Lars Doucet on Astral Codex Ten](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/your-book-review-progress-and-poverty).
