[Cove](https://cove.dev) is building AI-powered infrastructure that enables distribution owners to launch their own financial products in minutes — without code, capital, or compliance headaches — and keep all the margin.

As CTO, I led the technical vision, architecture, and engineering at cove. Beyond building the platform, I worked directly with customers to architect and ship embedded fintech integrations tailored to their products.

#### What we're building

Launching a financial product traditionally takes years of engineering, regulatory work, and capital. Cove collapses that to minutes. Distribution owners describe the product they want, and our platform handles the rest — from regulatory compliance to go-to-market infrastructure.

> "We tried to build this internally for 3 years. Cove got us to market in 6 weeks."

#### Key deliverables

- Took the company from $0 to hundreds of thousands in ARR and from $0 to tens of millions in valuation
- Architected and shipped the core platform end-to-end
- Built and released a JavaScript SDK and external API for distribution partners
- Shipped a Chrome extension for streamlined partner workflows
- Designed and deployed AI agents for automating financial operations, including a voice calling agent powered by ElevenLabs
- Built a PDF generation pipeline with Gotenberg for compliance and reporting
