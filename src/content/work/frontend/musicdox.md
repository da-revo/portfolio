**MusicDox** is a live, synced music editing platform — Google Docs for music production. Collaborate in real-time, share your creative process, and perform live with friends or bandmates across the globe.

Built at [Hack the 6ix 2024](https://devpost.com/software/musicdox), Toronto. I built all the core functionality — real-time sync with Socket.io, the audio engine with HowlerJS, and the editing interface in Next.js.
